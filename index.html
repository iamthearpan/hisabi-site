Hisabi

A simple, offline accounts book for Android.

Hisabi helps you keep track of money between you and the people you work with. Organise everything as Contacts → Sites → IDs → Entries, record payments received and paid, and see at a glance who needs to pay whom.

What it does

  • Keeps contacts, sites, IDs and entries in one place
  • Calculates the client share and the balance for every entry automatically
  • Records payments received and paid, with a running ledger per contact
  • Creates PDF statements you can share
  • Works fully offline – your data stays on your phone
  • Optional backup and restore to your own Google Drive

Your data

Hisabi has no accounts and no servers. If you turn on Google Drive backup, a single backup file is stored in a private, hidden app folder in your own Google Drive. Read the Privacy Policy for details.
