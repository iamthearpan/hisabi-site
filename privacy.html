Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This policy explains how the Hisabi Android app ("Hisabi", "the app") handles information. Hisabi is built to keep your accounts data under your control.

1. Data you enter

Hisabi stores the contacts, sites, IDs, entries, payments and notes that you type in. This data is saved only on your device. We do not operate any server, and we do not receive, collect, sell or share this data.

2. Google Drive backup (optional)

If you choose to connect your Google account, Hisabi can back up and restore your data using Google Drive.

  • What is accessed: the app requests only the drive.appdata permission. This lets Hisabi create, read and delete its own backup file in a private, hidden "App Data" folder in your Google Drive. Hisabi cannot see or access any of your other Drive files.
  • Where the backup lives: in your own Google Drive. It is not sent to the developer or to any third party.
  • Your Google account: Hisabi uses Google Sign-In only to obtain permission for the backup. The sign-in details are handled by Google and Android; the developer does not receive your password.
  • Control: you can disconnect at any time in the app, and you can remove the backup or revoke access from your Google Account settings (myaccount.google.com/permissions).

Hisabi's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Other backups and exports

The app can export a backup file or a PDF statement to a location you choose, and share it through apps you pick. Those files are handled by you and the apps you share them with.

4. What we do not do

  • No advertising and no ad identifiers
  • No analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services
  • No sale or sharing of personal data

5. Permissions

  • Contacts (optional): if you tap the option to pick a person from your phone, Hisabi asks for permission to read your phone's contacts so you can choose a name and number to fill in. This is read on your device only. Your phone contacts are not uploaded, stored on any server, or shared. If you deny the permission you can still type details in manually.
  • Internet: used only for Google Drive backup and restore.
  • Background activity: if you connect Google Drive, Hisabi may run briefly in the background (usually at night) to check whether a backup is due and, if so, upload it.
  • Notifications (optional): used only to show the progress and result of a backup.
  • Storage (older Android versions): used only to save backup or PDF files you export.

Hisabi does not access your location, camera, microphone or messages.

6. Children

Hisabi is a business tool and is not directed to children.

7. Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app or using its erase option removes the data stored on your device. A Google Drive backup, if you made one, can be removed by disconnecting in the app or from your Google Account.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
